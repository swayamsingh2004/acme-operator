# AP-04 — Vendor Inquiries

Vendors ask "where is my payment?" every day. Answer within one business day, from the AP mailbox, by **replying to the vendor's email**.

## 1. Investigate first
1. Find every invoice the vendor mentions in **Ledgerly → Invoices**, using normalised invoice numbers (see AP-01 §2.2).
2. Read the status and, for holds, the hold reason. For paid invoices, take the payment date and payment reference.

## 2. What to tell the vendor

| Ledgerly status | Reply with |
|---|---|
| Paid | Payment date, amount, payment reference. |
| Approved | Approved and scheduled for payment on or before the due date. |
| Pending approval | Received and in our approval process; expected payment by the due date. |
| On hold | That the invoice is **on hold**, the business reason in plain words (e.g. "the invoiced amount exceeds the purchase order"), and what is needed from them (e.g. a credit note or corrected invoice, or confirmation from their account manager). |
| Not found | We have no record; ask them to send the invoice PDF to the AP mailbox. |

## 3. Do not disclose
- Internal approver names, internal notes, other vendors' information, bank details.

## 4. Internal follow-up
For **On hold** invoices, also email Procurement (`purchasing@acme-robotics.example`) that the vendor is chasing it, so the hold is resolved. Move the vendor's email to *Processed* after replying.
